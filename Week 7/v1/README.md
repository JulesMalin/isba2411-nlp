# Lecture 13 versions

Both builds are published side by side in the Week 7 folder. Students can use either;
v2 is the one taught.

| File | Slides | Runtime | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `L13_Support_Copilot_v1.pptx` | 25 | 91 min | The original build. Opens with tokens, then explains the transformer block over three slides, then retrieval, routing and the shipping decision. |
| `L13_Support_Copilot_v2_compressed.pptx` | 21 | 90 min | Leads with semantic search *working*, then explains how. The transformer block is one callback slide to Lecture 11 rather than three slides of re-teaching. The time saved funds the in-class Shipping Decision game. |

v2 exists because v1 overlapped Lecture 11 too heavily: it opened where L11 opened and
re-taught the block. v2 keeps all the new material (retrieval, zero-shot, entailment,
confidence, the cost threshold) and cuts the repetition.

This folder holds the v1 builder scripts under `_source/` for reproducibility.

**Note:** the published decks have no speaker notes. The annotated copies stay with the
instructor.
